## Product implications
1. Support an **invoice-only audit mode**, separate from completed-work-versus-contract reconciliation.
2. Report valid invoice totals as verified arithmetic, and mark rate, duplicate-service and payment conclusions **not verifiable** when authoritative data is absent.
3. Treat economic price adjustment and environmental fees as **require contract evidence**, not erroneous.
4. For pilots, get customer authorization for a complete rate card, service/work export, invoices and payments.
5. Never approach named companies alleging billing mistakes based on incomplete public documents.

This is a provenance and public-data feasibility record, not a finding of wrongdoing.
